Heart

The heart is a muscular organ that pumps blood throughout the body.

📍

Location

The heart is located in the chest, between the lungs, slightly to the left of the center of the chest.

⚙️

Function

The heart pumps blood through the circulatory system. This supplies oxygen and nutrients to the body and helps remove carbon dioxide and other waste products.

🩺

Common Diseases

Common cardiovascular conditions include coronary artery disease, high blood pressure, heart failure, and some types of heart rhythm disorders.

🛡️

Prevention

Heart health can be supported by regular physical activity, a balanced diet, maintaining a healthy weight, avoiding tobacco, and managing blood pressure and cholesterol.

🔬

Scientific Fact

The heart is divided into four chambers: two atria and two ventricles. These chambers work together to move blood through the lungs and the rest of the body.
