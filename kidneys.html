Kidneys

The kidneys are two important organs that filter the blood, remove waste products, and help maintain the body's fluid and chemical balance.

📍

Location

The kidneys are located in the lower back area of the abdomen, on both sides of the spine and behind the abdominal organs.

⚙️

Function

The kidneys filter the blood, remove waste and excess water to produce urine, regulate electrolytes and blood pressure, and help control red blood cell production.

🩺

Common Diseases

Common kidney diseases include kidney stones, chronic kidney disease, kidney infections, glomerulonephritis, and kidney failure.

🛡️

Prevention

Kidney health can be protected by drinking enough water, maintaining a healthy diet, exercising regularly, controlling blood pressure and blood sugar, and avoiding excessive use of certain medicines.

🔬

Scientific Fact

Each kidney contains about one million tiny filtering units called nephrons, which help filter the blood and form urine.
