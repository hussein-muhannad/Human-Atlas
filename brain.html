Brain

The brain is the body's control center, responsible for thoughts, memory, emotions, movement, and vital body functions.

📍

Location

The brain is located inside the skull at the top of the head. It is protected by the skull, the meninges, and cerebrospinal fluid.

⚙️

Function

The brain controls the nervous system, processes sensory information, regulates movement, stores memories, controls emotions, and coordinates essential body functions such as breathing and heartbeat.

🩺

Common Diseases

Common brain disorders include stroke, Alzheimer's disease, Parkinson's disease, epilepsy, brain tumors, and meningitis.

🛡️

Prevention

Brain health can be maintained through regular exercise, a balanced diet, quality sleep, mental activities, avoiding smoking, limiting alcohol, and controlling blood pressure and diabetes.

🔬

Scientific Fact

The adult human brain contains approximately 86 billion neurons that communicate through trillions of synaptic connections, making it one of the most complex organs in the human body.
