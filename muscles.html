Muscles

Muscles are specialized tissues that allow the body to move. They also help maintain posture, stabilize joints, and produce heat to keep the body warm.

📍

Location

Muscles are found throughout the body and are attached to bones, organs, and other tissues. Skeletal muscles are mainly located around the bones and joints.

⚙️

Function

Muscles allow movement, maintain body posture and balance, support joints, move substances through internal organs, and generate heat through contraction.

🩺

Common Diseases

Common muscle conditions include muscle strains, muscular dystrophy, myositis, muscle cramps, and other disorders that can affect muscle strength and movement.

🛡️

Prevention

Muscle health can be supported by regular physical activity, proper nutrition, staying hydrated, warming up before exercise, getting enough rest, and using correct exercise techniques.

🔬

Scientific Fact

The human body has more than 600 muscles. Skeletal muscles work with bones and joints to produce voluntary movements such as walking, running, and lifting.
