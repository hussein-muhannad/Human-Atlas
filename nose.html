Nose

The nose is an important part of the respiratory and sensory systems. It allows us to breathe, detect smells, and helps filter, warm, and humidify the air we breathe.

📍

Location

The nose is located in the center of the face. It connects the outside environment with the nasal cavity and the upper respiratory tract.

⚙️

Function

The nose helps air enter the respiratory system, filters dust and particles, warms and humidifies the air, and contains receptors responsible for the sense of smell.

🩺

Common Diseases

Common nose problems include sinusitis, allergic rhinitis, nasal polyps, nosebleeds, and infections such as the common cold.

🛡️

Prevention

Nose health can be protected by avoiding cigarette smoke and harmful pollutants, washing hands regularly, staying hydrated, and managing allergies properly.

🔬

Scientific Fact

The human nose contains millions of smell receptors that help detect and distinguish different odors.
