Bones

Bones are strong structures that form the skeleton of the human body. They provide support, protect internal organs, allow movement, and store important minerals.

📍

Location

Bones are found throughout the body and are connected together to form the skeleton, including the skull, spine, ribs, arms, legs, and pelvis.

⚙️

Function

Bones support the body, protect organs such as the brain and heart, work with muscles to produce movement, store minerals, and contain bone marrow that produces blood cells.

🩺

Common Diseases

Common bone conditions include osteoporosis, bone fractures, osteoarthritis, bone infections, and bone cancer.

🛡️

Prevention

Bone health can be supported by getting enough calcium and vitamin D, exercising regularly, maintaining a healthy weight, avoiding smoking, and preventing falls and injuries.

🔬

Scientific Fact

An adult human skeleton usually contains 206 bones. Bones are living tissues that continuously change and repair themselves throughout life.
