Lungs

The lungs are the main organs of the respiratory system. They supply oxygen to the body and remove carbon dioxide.

📍

Location

The lungs are located inside the chest on both sides of the heart. They are protected by the rib cage and separated by the mediastinum.

⚙️

Function

The lungs exchange oxygen and carbon dioxide between the air and the bloodstream. They also help regulate blood pH and support speech by providing airflow.

🩺

Common Diseases

Common lung diseases include asthma, pneumonia, chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD), lung cancer, and tuberculosis.

🛡️

Prevention

Lung health can be protected by avoiding smoking, exercising regularly, reducing exposure to air pollution, getting recommended vaccinations, and maintaining good hygiene.

🔬

Scientific Fact

The lungs contain approximately 300 million alveoli, tiny air sacs where oxygen enters the blood and carbon dioxide leaves the body.
