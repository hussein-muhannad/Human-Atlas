Eye

The eye is a complex sensory organ that allows us to see the world around us by detecting light and sending visual information to the brain.

📍

Location

The eyes are located in the bony eye sockets of the skull, called the orbits, and are positioned at the front of the head.

⚙️

Function

The eyes detect light, colors, shapes, and movement. They convert light into electrical signals that are sent through the optic nerve to the brain for processing.

🩺

Common Diseases

Common eye diseases include cataracts, glaucoma, age-related macular degeneration, conjunctivitis, and diabetic retinopathy.

🛡️

Prevention

Eye health can be protected by having regular eye examinations, wearing protective eyewear, eating a healthy diet, managing screen time, and protecting the eyes from excessive sunlight.

🔬

Scientific Fact

The human eye can distinguish millions of different colors and works together with the brain to create the images we see.
