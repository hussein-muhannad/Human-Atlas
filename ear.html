Ear

The ear is a sensory organ responsible for hearing and maintaining balance. It receives sound waves and converts them into signals that the brain can understand.

📍

Location

The ears are located on both sides of the head. Each ear includes the outer ear, middle ear, and inner ear, which work together for hearing and balance.

⚙️

Function

The ears allow us to hear sounds and help maintain balance and body position through structures in the inner ear.

🩺

Common Diseases

Common ear problems include ear infections, hearing loss, tinnitus, impacted earwax, and disorders of the inner ear.

🛡️

Prevention

Ear health can be protected by avoiding very loud sounds, using hearing protection when necessary, keeping the ears clean safely, and treating ear infections properly.

🔬

Scientific Fact

The smallest bones in the human body are found in the middle ear. They are called the malleus, incus, and stapes and help transmit sound vibrations.
