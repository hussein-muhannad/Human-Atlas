Stomach

The stomach is an important organ of the digestive system. It stores food, mixes it with digestive juices, and helps break it down into smaller substances.

📍

Location

The stomach is located in the upper left part of the abdomen, below the diaphragm and next to the liver and spleen.

⚙️

Function

The stomach stores food and mixes it with acids and digestive enzymes. It helps break down proteins and gradually releases food into the small intestine.

🩺

Common Diseases

Common stomach diseases include gastritis, stomach ulcers, gastroesophageal reflux disease (GERD), stomach infections, and stomach cancer.

🛡️

Prevention

Stomach health can be supported by eating a balanced diet, maintaining good hygiene, avoiding smoking, limiting excessive alcohol, and using medicines safely.

🔬

Scientific Fact

The stomach can expand to hold a large amount of food and uses strong muscular contractions to mix food with digestive juices.
