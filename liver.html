Liver

The liver is one of the largest organs in the human body. It performs many important functions, including processing nutrients, removing toxins, and producing substances needed for digestion and blood clotting.

📍

Location

The liver is located in the upper right side of the abdomen, below the diaphragm and above the stomach, right kidney, and intestines.

⚙️

Function

The liver helps process nutrients, produces bile to aid digestion, stores energy, regulates blood chemicals, and removes harmful substances from the blood.

🩺

Common Diseases

Common liver diseases include hepatitis, fatty liver disease, cirrhosis, liver cancer, and liver failure.

🛡️

Prevention

Liver health can be protected by maintaining a healthy weight, eating a balanced diet, exercising regularly, avoiding excessive alcohol, preventing hepatitis infections, and using medicines safely.

🔬

Scientific Fact

The liver is the largest internal organ in the human body and can regenerate itself after a significant portion is removed or damaged.
