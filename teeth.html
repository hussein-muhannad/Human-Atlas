Teeth

Teeth are hard structures in the mouth that help us bite, chew, and break down food. They also play an important role in speech and maintaining the shape of the face.

📍

Location

The teeth are located inside the mouth and are held in the upper and lower jaws by supporting tissues called the gums and periodontal structures.

⚙️

Function

Teeth help bite and chew food into smaller pieces, making it easier to swallow and digest. They also help form sounds and support the structure of the face.

🩺

Common Diseases

Common dental problems include tooth decay, gum disease, tooth infections, cavities, tooth sensitivity, and tooth loss.

🛡️

Prevention

Teeth can be protected by brushing twice a day with fluoride toothpaste, flossing regularly, limiting sugary foods and drinks, and visiting a dentist for regular checkups.

🔬

Scientific Fact

Most adults have 32 permanent teeth, including incisors, canines, premolars, and molars. Tooth enamel is the hardest substance in the human body.
